Cookie Policy

Welcome to Zelux Beam! To improve your experience with our IT platform, ensure its proper functioning, and analyze user activity, we use cookies and similar technologies. This policy explains in detail what technologies we use, for what purposes, and how you can manage them.

What are cookies?

A cookie is a small piece of data (text file) that a website stores on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit it. This allows the platform to "remember" your actions and preferences (such as login, language, font size, and other settings) over a period of time, so you don't have to re-enter them on every visit.

What types of cookies do we use?

We classify the cookies used on the Zelux Beam website into the following categories:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: These cookies are critical for the operation of the educational platform. They allow you to navigate the site and use its basic features.
  • Analytical and Performance Cookies: These cookies collect anonymous information about how visitors use our site. They help us understand which courses are most popular and how users interact with the content.
  • Functional Cookies: These cookies allow the site to remember your choices (such as language settings or region) to provide a more personalized educational experience.
  • Advertising (Marketing) Cookies: These cookies are used to deliver advertisements that are most relevant to your interests in the field of IT training.

Why do we use cookies?

The main purposes of using cookies on the Zelux Beam platform are:

  • Ensuring stable and secure operation of your personal account.
  • Analyzing user behavior to optimize the structure of educational materials.
  • Personalizing content and settings for student convenience.
  • Providing relevant offers for training programs.

Your Choice and How to Manage Cookies

You have full control over cookies. You can change your settings in your browser or through our Cookie Popup at any time. Most modern browsers allow you to view, delete, and block cookies. However, please note: disabling strictly necessary cookies may lead to the malfunction of some sections of the school.
