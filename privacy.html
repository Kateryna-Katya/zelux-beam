Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to personal data provided by users of the Zelux Beam platform to receive educational services, participate in events, and for any other interaction, usually in electronic format (via the website, messengers, or email).

Zelux Beam reserves the right to make changes and additions to the current provisions of this Policy. Please note that our services may contain links to third-party resources that have their own data processing rules. In such cases, our platform is not responsible for the content or compliance with the privacy policies of third-party companies. By providing your personal data, you give full consent to its processing by the methods provided for in this Policy.

Data Collection, Storage, and Erasure Procedures

Zelux Beam collects personal data only with the user's consent and solely to provide access to educational materials, consultations, and technical support. To prevent data leaks, we use a full range of information security measures that comply with EU standards.

The personal data storage period is the period necessary to provide educational services, but not exceeding the period established by current legislation. Destruction, depersonalization, or blocking of data is carried out at the user's request or upon fulfillment of the processing purposes.

Categories of Collected Data

1. Technical Information:

  • Access time and device IP address;
  • Referral sources to the platform;
  • Visited pages and views of educational blocks;
  • Technical data of the browser and operating system;
  • Phone number (in case of requesting a callback).

2. Information for Learning Personalization:

When registering or filling out an application form, we collect data necessary for authentication and feedback:

  • User's first and last name;
  • Email address and contact phone number.

Purposes of Data Processing

  • Providing access to courses and mentoring support;
  • Accounting for preferences when developing new educational programs;
  • Informing about new technologies, discounts, and webinars (with opt-out possibility);
  • Ensuring prompt feedback on educational issues.

Data Transfer to Third Parties

We do not transfer your data to third parties, except in the following cases:

  • Presence of your explicit consent;
  • Upon official request from law enforcement agencies within the framework of the law;
  • In case of reorganization or merger of the company.

Use of Cookies

We use cookies to analyze traffic and save your settings (for example, language selection or authorization status). This allows us to make the platform more convenient. You can disable Cookies at any time in your browser settings; however, this may limit access to some functions of the school.

Your Rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, or delete it. If you wish to stop processing your personal data or withdraw your consent, please contact us at: support@zelux-beam.blog.
