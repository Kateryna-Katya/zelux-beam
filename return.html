Return Policy

Refund Conditions

We strive to provide the highest quality of education on the Zelux Beam platform. However, if the program does not suit you, you may be eligible for a full or partial refund in the following situations:

  • Course Mismatch: If the content of the provided course differs significantly from the program described on our website at the time of purchase.
  • Technical Failures on Our Side: In the event of critical technical issues on the Zelux Beam platform that make learning impossible and were not resolved by our team within a reasonable timeframe (more than 72 hours).
  • Right of Withdrawal ("Cooling-off Period"): In accordance with EU norms, if you decide to withdraw from the course within 14 (fourteen) calendar days from the moment of payment, provided that you have not yet started active learning (see "Exceptions" section).

Refund Request Procedure

To initiate a refund procedure, please follow these steps:

  1. Draft an email and send it to our official email: support@zelux-beam.blog.
  2. In the subject line, be sure to include: "Refund Request: [Your Name]".
  3. In the body of the email, specify: your first and last name, the email used during registration, and the course name.
  4. Describe the reason for the refund, referring to the relevant clause of our terms.
  5. Our support team will review the request within 3 business days.

Refund Processing Time and Method

Once the request is approved, the refund will be processed within 7–14 business days. Funds are returned using the same method used for the payment (to the same bank card or account). Please note that the actual credit time depends on your bank's regulations.

Exceptions and Limitations

A refund cannot be issued if:

  • The request is submitted after 14 calendar days from the date of payment.
  • You have already studied or downloaded more than 50% of the educational module materials.
  • The reason for the inability to study is technical problems on the user's side (provider issues, outdated software).
  • Your account was blocked for violating platform rules or copyrights.

Policy Updates

Zelux Beam reserves the right to update this Policy. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes your agreement to the new terms.

Any questions left? Write to us: support@zelux-beam.blog
